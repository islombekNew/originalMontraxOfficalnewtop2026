## The problem

Small retail shops in Uzbekistan still keep their books in notebooks or Excel. Stock levels are fuzzy, customer debts get forgotten, and compiling a monthly report takes a full day. Off-the-shelf CRMs are either too expensive or don't match local trade habits — the debt ledger, mixed cash-and-credit payments.

## The solution

Montrax Savdo is a system built from scratch for local retail. It drops the bloat of big CRMs and makes the things a seller touches every day fast and simple:

- Product catalog — stock tracking with low-stock alerts
- Sales screen — a sale in a few clicks, mixed payment (cash + credit)
- Customer debt ledger — who owes what, since when, with full history
- Reports — daily/monthly sales, Excel export
- Soft-delete — accidentally removed records can be restored

## Design decisions

This is where having the designer and the developer in one head paid off: the interface isn't a "pretty admin template" — it's drawn around how a real seller actually moves.

- **Big buttons, big numbers** — it's used in a hurry, on a shop counter; delicate UI doesn't survive there
- **Dark theme** — a screen that stays on until late evening shouldn't tire the eyes
- **One screen — one task** — the seller never has to wonder "where am I?"

## Outcome

The system shipped to production in July 2026 and is used in live trade. Debt tracking moved from the notebook into the system — one search now pulls up a customer's entire history.
